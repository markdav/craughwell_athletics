The sun shone through another fantastically busy week in Irish Athletics as the highlight of the Juvenile Track and Field calendar came to a close.  Irelands largest two International Track Meets took place this week too, and each saw Craughwell AC Competitors.  Finally, the Galway Senior Track and Field took place in Dangan on Sunday.

Cork City Sports on Wednesday kicked things off with some great action.  Conor Penney won Silver in the men's High Jump, clearing a superb 2.05m and narrowly failing on the next height three times.  The event also saw a welcome return to action for Evan Hallinan who cleared 1.90m for 4th.  Shauna Bocquet came 2nd in the Leisureworld T54 800m, with 2:01.87.

On Friday, Shauna was again in action at the Morton Games where she finished 2ns in a faster time of 1:57.32, despite an issue with her strap.

Saturday was once again spent in Tullamore in the baking heat with some fantastic performances.  Lauren Kilduff won a sensational Gold in the 400m Hurdles, covering the distance in a blistering 1:00.33.  Lauren was joined on the podium by her good friend and training partner Emma O'Donovan in a great display of force from the training group.  U15 Carl Morris came 9th in the 250m Hurdles in 41.59 and Faye Whelan 12th in (40.80).  ?U16 Alan Warde came a battling 9th in the 250m Hurdles with 38.10.

The high jump is always a strong discipline for Craughwell and this year was no exception.  Darragh Kelly won Gold in the U19 boys High Jump with a fine 1.75m for his last Juvenile competition.  Charlie Daniels sprang to 5.48m in the U16 boys long Jump to come 6th in the large field.

Halle Feerick showed a blistering turn of pace to win the U14 Boys 200m with a fine 25.08.  He clocked 24.83 to win his semi!  Some other strong sprint results included Sean Hoade who was 7th in the u18 200m (22.70), with Toyosi George 11th (23.10), Cian Brady 13th (23.10) and Donnacha Lawless 21st (24.09).    In the U15 girls, , Grace Drysdale came 10th (28.18) with Adele Casserly 11th. In the boys, Koshi Tamura was 15th in 27.07.  Aeveen Kelly came 15th in the U17 girls 200m clocking a fine 27.26.  Lauren Kelly was 7th in the U14 Girls 200m with 27.50.

There were some strong outings from the 1500m boys.   David Frazer arrived in from holidays that morning, but paced his race to perfection to come 5th in the U16 Boys 1500m in 4:18.37.  Latter in the U18 race, his training partner Cathal Gallagher ran an almost identically paced effort to finish with 4:19.4 in 19th place overall.

Sunday was the turn of the relays and the "B" all-Irelands.   The u18 boys team of Cian Brady, Toyosi George, Sean Hoade, Donnacha Lawless and Conor Penney won Bronze with a club record 44.92s clocking.  The U18 girls of Lauren Kilduff, Emma O'Donovan, Aeveen Kelly, Medan Devane and Emily Nestor clocked 50.41 for 4th, also a club Senior record.  The U14 Girls team were also an agonising 4th in 53.98 with Penny Forde, Ruby Maher, Laoise Colleran, Lauren Kelly, Ella White and Maeve Desbonnet.  The boys were 7th in 52.27 with  their crew of Kai Masterson, Matiss Ozolins, Erik Daniels, Halle Feerick, Cormac Burke and Eoin Trainor.  

There were some good results in the B All-Ireland with U13s Charlie Looby and Aaron Cummins winning Gold in the High Jump and Long Jump respectively.  Laoise Scully won Bronze in the U16 girls 800m in a fine 2:28.61.  Fine performance also in U12 Boys Long Jump from Oran Gillespie  (23rd, 3.24), the U13 Boys 80m Sprint: Matthew Drysdale (7th, 11.81), U13 Girls 600m Ruth Gogarty (22nd, 2:22.01), U13 Girls Long Jump Ruth Gogarty (27th, 2.22), Darragh Munnelly (8th U15 Boys 800m 2:26.17). and Isabelle Power (4th U16 Girls Long Jump 4.44).

On Saturday closer to home was the Galway Junior and Senior track and field Championships and Craughwell AC Athletes were dominant.  We saw Gold-medal winning performances from Oisin Davis (800m, 1.57), Declan O'Connell (1500m, 4.00.35), William Fitzgerald (3000m, 8:40), Cathal Gallagher (JM 3000m 9:24), Anna McKernan (1500mm 5:35), Sinead Treacy (400m, 55.91), Jade Moorhead (HJ, 1.55) and Siona Lawless (LJ 4.61).  Jordan Walsh won Silver in the 3000m in 9:22.
