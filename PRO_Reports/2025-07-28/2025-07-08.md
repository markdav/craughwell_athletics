Conor Penney put in another fantastic performance in the European Youth Olympics High Jump final last Thursday with a super string of clearances and a best of 2.04 placing him 6th in Europe!

Conor entered the competition at 1.90m and cleared comfortably. A knock on his first attempt at 1.95m following by a slight adjustment of the mark and a huge clearance on his second attempt. Conor proceeded to clear 1.98, 2.01 and 2.04 all on his first attempt with room to spare.

2.07 proved to be the barrier today and Conor went very close on his first 2 attempts, just brushing it with his heels each time. Conor has high ambitions and although he was a little disappointed not to be attempting a PB, placing 6th in Europe against the best from the continent and in the heated caldron of the Todor Proeski Stadium in Skopje, was such a fantastic achievement and makes the club, his coaches and his family extremely proud.

Closer to home, the National League Finals took place in Tullamore and County Galway were again to the fore under team managers Siobhan Kelleher (men) and Sarah Finnegan (women).  

The men won Gold for the 3rd year in a row, sharing the podium with Donegal and Kerry - fitting for All-Ireland finals day.  There were several Craughwell names among the scorers.  Justin Lane is the inspirational captain of the men's team and won the pole Vault in a fine 2.30m clearance and was 5th in the WFD.  Oisín Davis won the 1500m in a high quality contest. getting passed by an East-Cork rival with 200m to go but he kept his cool to pass him again and win with 3:53.21.  Darragh Kelly won the High Jump in an excellent competition where the first three cleared an identical 1.80m.  Keelan Moorhead blitzed a strong 53.49 for 5th in the Men's 400m and also ran strongly in the 4x400m relay.  

The team scores were boosted by strong performances from Abas Edris of Castlegar (1st Steeple), Ciaran Kelleher South Galway (2nd 1500m Walk), West Coast AC's Leon Ewere (1st 200m) and Luciano Lemar 1st 100m), Darragh Fahy (1st TJ, 2nd LJ), Athenry AC's talismanic thrower Liam Shaw was 1st in both Shot and Discus.

Sarah Finnegan managed the women's team and they won Silver in another fine performance with plenty of Craughwell.  Sinéad Treacy is always willing to row in on every Galway team and she delivered another class performance to win the 400m in a fine 55.39s, a good sign before Nationals next weekend.  Leanagh Gilligan cleared 9.83 for 2nd in the TJ with  Alisha Larkin clearing 5m for 2nd in the LJ.  Jade Moorhead cleared 1.55m for 3rd in the High Jump.  Arlene Earls took a late call-up to run a fine Season's best 2:23.6 for 5th in the 800m.  Craughwell Sprinters were to the fore in the 4x100 and 4x400m relay teams who each took the maximum points.  

Again, it was a multi-club affair, with strong performances from Alix Joyce (1st Steeple), Laura Nally (1st 100m), Nicole Walsh (1st 200m) helping the team to Silver.

On Saturday it was the turn of the Masters Athletes in the National Masters T&F Finals.  There were two competitors in action form Craughwell AC - Sarah Finnegan won Gold in the W35 Triple Jump with 8.5m, and Martin Curley won Bronze in the M35 Triple Jump, clearing 9.65m.
