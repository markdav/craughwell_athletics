The best student distance runners in Ireland descended on Tallaght on Saturday for the IUAA Cross Country Championships.  The venue was a fine dry technical course, with tight turns and a few short sharp hills.  

There were some fantastic performances from Craughwell AC athletes representing their Universities and Colleges.  Oisín Davis won individual Silver in the men's race after a great battle with his teammate and friend Oisín Murray, covering the ~7.8km in 24.09.  Mathys Bocquet, representing UL, was next Craughwell man home in 28th and Anthony Boyle in 75th in the 140-strong field despite a stomach bug.  Sean Cotter had to pull up with a niggle.  The University of Galway Team came 3rd overall.

Anna McKernan got the late call up to University of Galway and ran fantastically to finish 57th.  Her clubmate Anna Cotter was close behind in 66th for Trinity.  Anna's University of Galway women's team also won Bronze

After the distance events, there was a mixed relay of 4x1500m and a Craughwell heavy team featuring Kyle Moorhead and Paddy Noonan, running as Graduates, came 2nd to claim another medal for University of Galway.

The Galway Uneven-ages Championships took place in Tuam in the challlenging course of Pallace grounds.  

The U9 Girls team of Lara McGovern (3), Meabh Galvin (6), Aoife Hardiman (8), Lauren Lydon (15), Caoimhe Hall, Lena Duval de Dampierre Phelan, Caoileadh Staunton and Sadhbh Fahy did brilliantly to win Gold.  The U9 boys were not to be outdone and the lads team of Alex McGovern (4), Daithi Colleran (6), Cian Monahan (8), Dara O'Mahony (12) and Gavin Whiriskey blitzed to Gold with low score of 30 points.

The U11 crew had a massive turnout and Katie Considine (1), Amelie Glynn (6), Chloe Lydon (10), Aisling Cotter (17), Eabha Quinn, Ailbhe Kearney, Robyn Kelly and Kate Meehan blazed to another Gold team medal, with Katie winning individual Gold.  The U11 Boys won Bronze with the crew of Oisin Phelan, Billy O'Brien, Hugo Kinsella, Leo Hardiman, Liam O'Flynn, Henry McHugh, Cuan Deely, Gabriel Killoran, Robert McMahon, Sean Walsh, and Conor Keating packing well.

Another fantastic Gold for the U13 girls team of Emma Mc Govern (2), Muireann Kelly (4), Aoife O'Connell (5), Leah O'Toole (8), Naoise Quinn, Elsie May Earls, Freya Wholihan, Jenny Hannon and Kate Keenan.  Paul Egan and Tom Clarke ran well in the U13 Boys race to come 23rd and 30th respectively.

Sophie Meehan stepped up to come 6th in the U15 Girls race, with Darragh Munnelly coming 9th. Aisling Cosgrove came a fine 5th in the U17 Girls race.   Fergus Kelly has been flying this season and came 4th in the Intermediates.
