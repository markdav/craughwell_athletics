William Fitzgerald delivered another headline-grabbing performance on Sunday by coming 2nd in the prestigious Autumn Open International Cross Country in Abbotstown.  The Peterswell man had a good block of training in but flew under the radar in the build-up.  The hilly course was decently muddy in places but mostly good underfoot.  

Callum Morgan of Candour Track Club has a 13.23 5km from this year and justified his favourite tag by making a break with 5km left to run.  William felt it was too early to make his move so worked in the leading pack of 15 athletes until a little over half way when he made his own break.  William put about 40m on the top-class chasing pack but wasn't able to close the gap to Morgan.  In the end, William came a fantastic second, the highest ever finish in the competition by a Galwayman and well clear of some amazing athletes including several sub-4 minute milers and sub-14 5km runners.

Back in Loughrea - there were some fantastic performances from Craughwell AC Athletes in the Connacht Schools Cross County finals.  Some highlight results included a famous win in the 3rd and 4thclass girls team for Gaelscoil De Hide, starring Chloe Ni Loideain (12), Amelie Nic Fhloinn (17), Gwen Ni Chongaile (19), Aisling Cotter (34), Katie Considine (42) and Lucy Ni Samhrain (72).  

Ciara Ward and Kate Whirskey blitzing the top-10 in the 5th and 6th class girls.  Oranmore (2nd) and Ballyglass (3rd) finished on the podium, which many Craughwell AC Athletes.   Jack Hibbett did the same in the 5th & 6th class boys race.
