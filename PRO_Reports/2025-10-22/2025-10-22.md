# Here is some markdown
Callum Morgan of Candour Track Club has a 13.23 5km from this year and justified his favourite tag by making a break with 5km left to run.  William felt it was too early to make his move so worked in the leading pack of 15 athletes until a little over half way when he made his own break.  William put about 40m on the top-class chasing pack but wasn't able to close the gap to Morgan.  In the end, William came a fantastic second, the highest ever finish in the competition by a Galwayman and well clear of some amazing athletes including several sub-4 minute milers and sub-14 5km runners.
