The Connacht Uneven ages Cross Country took place on Sunday morning in Ballinrobe Racecourse. The weather was bright and the footing good as the finest Athletes from the West competed over various distances.  

The Senior Men's race was a fantastic battle with a large fields of nearly 50 entrants.  Craughwell's U20 Mathys Bocquet managed had a fantastic battle at the front of affairs and won Silver in the end with a strong finish. Fergus Kelly finished 13th in another strong run.  Aimee Carr finished a strong 8th in her race after a fine battle with Holly Shaughnessy of Tuam.

Luke O'Sullivan ran well in the U19 boys to come 4th - a good return to form after a hard to shake injury earlier in the year.  

One of the runs of the day came from David Frazer who finished 3rd in the U17 Boys race, stepping up in age.  The two lads who just ahead of David were national medallists in u16 last year so he is in a good position with two weeks to go to nationals.  Davis was 3rd scorer on the winning Galway team.

Darragh Munnelly finished well up in the U15s with a fine 24th place finish in hte large field.

The Craughwell AC result of the day was a fantastic performance from the U13 ladies.  The rapidly improving Muireann Kelly blitzed home in 8th, closely followed by Emma Mc Govern in 10th and Aoife O'Connell in 11th and Leah O'Toole packed right behind in 18th.  The reserve athletes were Naoise Quinn (26th) and Elsie May Earls (54).  The girls also helped Galway county to a super Silver.

There was another fantastic medal by the U11 Girls.  Katie Considine has been flying all year and she was again to the fore here with a fine 4th place finish

The U11 boys also ran well, led by Oisin Phelan (32), Henry McHugh (46), Hugo Kinsella (49), Liam O'Flynn (81) and Darragh O'Callaghan (92).

Lauren Kelly (St Raphaels) and Faye Whelan (Pres Athenry) were in outstanding form in the Minor Girls grade at the Irish Schools Pentathlon Championships held in Athlone at the weekend with Lauren racing to victory in the 60m hurdles in an impressive 9.35 and also sharing first place in the high jump with a leap of 1.58m. Lauren followed this up with 4.25m in the long jump and 6.75m in the shot before blasting around the track in the final event of the day with an impressive 2:37.47 in the 800m to take 6th place overall in the event with 2804 points and still young enough for same age bracket next year. Faye was equally impressive to take 7th place overall with scores of 4.57m in the long jump, 7.51m in the shot, 1.46m in the high jump, 9.74 in the hurdles and 2:42.13 in the 800m for a total of 2701 points and several PBs.

There were strong performances also in the Junior Boys grade with Sean Cleare (Calasanctius Oranmore) placing 12th with 2125 points (24.92 200m, 4.86m LJ, 2:23.54 800m, 8.30m shot and 10.99 60mh) and Charlie Daniels (Clarin College) placing 14th with 1883 points (26.07 200m, 4.94m LJ, 9.34m shot and 9.97 60mh); and in the Intermediate Boys grade where Oisin O'Donovan (St Bridgids) placed 14th with 2204 points (26.56 200m, 4.77m LJ, 2:20.31 800m, 8.94m shot and 10.14 hurdles).
