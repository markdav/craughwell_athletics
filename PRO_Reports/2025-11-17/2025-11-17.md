The Irish University Athletic Association is one of the main development paths for Irish Athletes and their competiions are always a fun, competitive display of the best young student athletes in Ireland with rich history and long-standing rivalries.  Last Saturday the road relay competition took place in the grounds of Maynooth University.

In the road relays, the teams run laps of a little over 1 mile in distance.  The men's event had 5-legs with 1-mile, 2-mile, 3-mile, 2-mile and 1-mile legs.  Craughwell AC's Oisín Davis and Kyle Moorhead were part of the University of Galway "A" team which won Silver in a battle with a strong UL team, with DCU close behind.  Davis took his team from 9th to 2nd in a strong run of 9:39 over two miles and the positions were consolidated from there with Moorhead finishing strongly.  

This is just the 4th time in the 38 years of the event that Galway have medalled, and all those 5 medals have come with Craughwell AC athletes on the team.  Seán Cotter ran a strong 2-mile leg for UoG "B" team, and Mathys Bocquet did likewise for UL "B" team.  This is likely the final event for Cotter and Moorhead to represent Galway after prestigious careers, they will be a big loss to the college, as athletes and leaders.   In the women's event, Anna McKernan ran very strongly for the UoG "C" team.

Next weekend a large contingent of Craughwell AC Athletes head to Derry for the National Even Ages and Senior Cross Country Championships. This event is the highlight of the national cross country calendar.
