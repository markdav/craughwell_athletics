The National Cross Country Course in Abbotstown was the destination for the Even age and Novices All-Ireland on Sunday.  Rumour had it that the course was quite cut up after hosting  the national Cycle-Cross Championships towards the end of last month and two weeks of heavy rain were unlikely to improve things!  Spirits were unfazed by the conditions as the toughest guys and girls from Craughwell headed east for the final big Cross Country Duel of the year.

The action kicked off with the under 11 girls who absolutely knocked it out of the park to come a superb 5th place.  Superstar Katie Considine led the team home in 7th overall despite stepping up in age, followed by Chloe Lydon (47th), Amelie Glynn (62), Aisling Cotter (89), Elise Kerins (92) and Eabha Quinn (161).  Katie and Chloe scored on the Galway team that won Silver and Katie on the Connacht team that won Gold. 

Inspired by the heroics of the U11s, the U13 girls each had incredible runs.  They were led home by Muireann Kelly in 14th place, Emma McGovern 34th, Aoife O'Connell (61), Leah O'Toole (84), Naoise Quinn (89) and Elsie May Earls (173).

David Frazer stepped up in age to take on the U17s.  This race was a fact finding mission to get used to stiffer competition and hilly courses ahead of his international next year in London.  David ran a cool race, easing into it and catching bodies all through the race.  He finished a brilliant 10th, making his second podium of the month.

In the final race of the day, Fergus Kelly found himself the last man standing as more and more Craughwell men couldn't make the startline of the National Novices.  The tough Clarinbridge man started out a little conservatively but motored through the muck to finish in a fine 135th position.
